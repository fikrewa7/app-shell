How the pick works
  • Six candidate frames are sampled across 8 %–88 % of the runtime to dodge intro fades and end cards.
  • Each frame is scored on detail, colour, and exposure — the highest-scoring one wins.
  • Black / flat frames are detected and pushed to the back, with automatic re-capture retries.

Nothing is uploaded. All processing happens in your browser.
